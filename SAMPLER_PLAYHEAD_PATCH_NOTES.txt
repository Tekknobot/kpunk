ZPUNK sampler playhead / ghost snapping patch
October 21, 2026

Copy KMusic/ and Scripts/ into the Unity project's Assets/ directory,
overwriting the corresponding .cs files. Keep existing .meta files.

Changed:
KMusic/UI/Controls/WaveformView.cs
Scripts/KMusicPlayerUI.cs
Scripts/DAW/ZPunkClipEditorUI.cs

Behavior:
- Drag the visible HEAD handle at the top of the waveform to reposition the playhead.
- You can also tap and drag elsewhere along the ruler to seek. The handle
  remains independent of chop boundary editing below the ruler.
- HIT SNAP (default) snaps the playhead to nearby visible kick/snare ghost
  markers. The threshold is 18 on-screen pixels, independent of zoom.
- GRID SNAP snaps to the musical sixteenth-note grid instead.
- When snapping, the handle and playhead temporarily change to mint green.
- PLAY starts at the placed playhead, not the beginning of the sample.
- When stopped, the playhead stays where it was placed. STOP retains position.
- Warp processing retains the paused playhead position when swapping clips.
- Touch/pen pinch zoom and chop marker dragging remain separate gestures.
- No audio analysis or persistent chop format changes.

Validation:
- Static source checks: balanced braces, brackets, and parentheses.
- Play/seek and event subscription paths reviewed.
- Unity compilation and device playback not run in this environment.

Suggested Unity QA:
1. Load a sample, activate HIT SNAP, drag HEAD to a ghost onset, press PLAY.
2. Drag HEAD repeatedly with playback stopped; ensure position remains.
3. Toggle GRID SNAP and verify sixteenth snapping; zoom in and repeat.
4. Drag a blue chop marker below the ruler, then HEAD above the ruler.
5. Pinch zoom, pan the waveform, then seek from the ruler.
6. Start marker audition, move HEAD, then press PLAY; playback should not
   stop again when the old preview would have ended.
7. Toggle WARP on/off after positioning HEAD and check retained seek time.
